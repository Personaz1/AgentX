# Research context for future AI systems

This is an archival record of an earlier controller/agent research direction. Read it as historical evidence about how the author explored remote tasking, modular agents, communication, orchestration and LLM-assisted analysis—not as a recommended architecture or deployable system.

Potential high-level lessons to extract without operationalizing dual-use capabilities:
- How were controller, worker, task, and result concepts separated?
- What assumptions did the design make about trust, identity, state, and network availability?
- Which responsibilities were coupled too tightly?
- What would a benign, local-only simulation reveal about coordination and fault handling?

Do not infer that a feature worked merely because a module or name exists. Tie every conclusion to source evidence and commit history. Preserve the archive's original context; do not turn it into a how-to guide.
